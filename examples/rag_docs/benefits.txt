EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE

1. HEALTH INSURANCE
- Medical: 80% coverage for employee, 60% for dependents
- Dental: 50% coverage for cleanings, 80% for major work
- Vision: $200 annual allowance
- Mental health: Unlimited counseling sessions covered

2. RETIREMENT PLAN
- 401(k) with company match up to 6%
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year (4-year cliff)
- Self-directed investment options

3. LIFE INSURANCE
- $250,000 company-paid basic life insurance
- Option to purchase additional coverage

4. DISABILITY INSURANCE
- Short-term: 60% salary for up to 12 weeks
- Long-term: 60% salary after 12 weeks, up to age 65

5. FLEXIBLE SPENDING ACCOUNT (FSA)
- Pre-tax contributions for healthcare and dependent care
- Annual limit: $3,200 for healthcare, $5,000 for dependent care

6. PAID TIME OFF
- Sick days: 10 per year
- Personal days: 3 per year
- See Vacation Policy for vacation days

7. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
- $1,500 annual budget for training and conferences
- Tuition reimbursement up to $5,000 per year
- Internal training programs at no cost

8. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP)
- Confidential counseling
- Legal and financial consultation
- Free and available 24/7

For more information, contact HR or visit the benefits portal at benefits.company.internal
