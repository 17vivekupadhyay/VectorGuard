COMPANY VACATION POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
All full-time employees are entitled to paid vacation days each calendar year.

2. VACATION DAYS ALLOTMENT
- First year (0-1 years): 15 days per year
- Second to fifth year (1-5 years): 20 days per year
- Sixth year and beyond (5+ years): 25 days per year
- Part-time employees: prorated based on hours worked

3. REQUESTING VACATION
- Submit vacation requests at least 2 weeks in advance
- Use the company HR portal to request time off
- Notify your direct manager
- Wait for approval from your department

4. CARRYOVER
- Unused days do not carry over to the next year
- Maximum 5 days can be carried over with manager approval
- Days must be used within 12 months of accrual

5. HOLIDAYS
Company recognizes the following holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas

6. RESTRICTIONS
- Vacation cannot be taken during critical project deadlines
- At least one manager must be available in your department
- Vacation is not paid out upon termination

7. REMOTE WORK
While on vacation, remote workers should set their status to "Out of Office"
and not check emails. This is a hard requirement for mental health.

For questions, contact HR at hr@company.internal
